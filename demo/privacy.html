TripleSumm demo

Privacy

This static research demo does not provide accounts and does not intentionally collect, store, or sell personal information. It does not use first-party analytics.

YouTube player

The page does not contact YouTube until you choose Accept and load YouTube video. After that choice, the page loads the selected YouTube video in the privacy-enhanced embedded player. Switching examples changes the video in that player. YouTube and Google may receive information such as your IP address, browser and device information, the referring site, cookies or similar technologies, and playback interactions.

Your choice is stored only in this browser tab's session storage under youtube-consent. It is used to avoid showing the same prompt again during the current tab session and is removed when that session ends.

Third-party policies

Use of the embedded player is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
